Help and support

Common questions

Learn about phone compatibility, profile updates and team orders.

How does a Oneinfo card work?

Oneinfo brings your professional details together in one shareable profile. A tap on a compatible phone or a camera scan opens it, so new contacts can explore your work and save your details.

Does the other person need an app?

No. Your profile opens in their phone's browser. They need an internet connection to view it, and can save your contact details from there.

Will it work with my phone?

Tap sharing works on compatible phones with contactless reading enabled. If a tap isn't available, use the phone's camera to scan the code on your card. An internet connection is needed to view the profile.

Can I update my details later?

Yes. Update your digital profile when your number, role, or links change. The address on your physical card stays the same.

Do I keep the card after sharing?

Yes. Keep your Oneinfo and use it for your next introduction. The other person opens the digital profile, so you don't need to hand the card over.

Can I order cards for my whole team?

Yes. Use the team enquiry form to tell us about your company and quantity. Oneinfo will contact you using the email you provide.
