QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Good to know
before you tap.

Does the other person need an app?+

No. The profile opens in the phone’s browser. NFC-compatible phones can tap; other phones can scan the printed QR code.

Can I change my details later?+

Yes. Your profile can be updated while the permanent public address encoded on the card remains the same.

Do I give the card away?+

No. Keep the card and use it repeatedly. The recipient saves your details from the digital profile.

What happens if a phone has no NFC?+

Every card also carries a QR code that opens the same profile, so the experience has a reliable fallback.

Can you make cards for a company team?+

Yes. The production system is designed around batches, individual card identities, and quality checks. Use the team quote form to describe your quantity and finish.
